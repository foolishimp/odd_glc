## Purpose

Define how `odd_glc` interprets lifecycle release/readiness and operational
feedback without becoming a release ledger, deployment system, incident
controller, or product-local runtime-return loop.

Release and feedback are lifecycle meanings over admitted GTL/ABG proof,
residual, continuation, and re-entry truth. They are not proof substitutes.

## Scope

This requirement governs:

- the first steel-thread release/readiness disposition;
- future generic release and feedback interpretation;
- downstream specialization boundaries for software release, deployment, and
  operational return.

The Hello World steel thread may end in release/readiness, repair, reprice, or
block. It does not create a release cut, deployment lane, install product, or
operational loop.

## Acceptance Criteria

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-001**: `odd_glc` release/readiness interpretation shall be a label or query over admitted GTL/ABG requirement, evidence, fold, residual, continuation, and disposition truth. It shall not maintain a writable release ledger or proof checklist as source authority.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-002**: The first Hello World steel thread may interpret an ABG disposition as release/readiness for the scenario. That interpretation shall not claim a release cut, deployment, install, runtime return, or operational acceptance unless a later requirement and design explicitly prices those surfaces.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-003**: Release/readiness proof shall cite replay-derived lifecycle truth, admitted evidence, source/release identity where relevant, and the active requirement projection. It shall not close from local command success, local files, comments, or human assertion alone.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-004**: `odd_glc` shall preserve the distinction between source project, release cut, product, install, artifact, and development product when interpreting release/readiness state.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-005**: Operational feedback shall enter lifecycle interpretation as admitted context, evidence, residual pressure, continuation, correction, re-entry, or STDO reprice truth. `odd_glc` shall not introduce a product-local incident controller or feedback queue as runtime authority.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-006**: If release/readiness proof is incomplete, the lifecycle state shall remain residual, blocked, deferred, or repriced according to ABG disposition and STDO authority. `odd_glc` shall not override missing proof with a local release decision.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-007**: Downstream programs may specialize release, packaging, deployment, runtime return, operations, and incident policy. Those policies shall remain downstream surfaces bound to GTL/ABG truth and shall not become generic odd_glc lifecycle law.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-008**: Future odd_sdlc specialization may add software release assets, deployment evidence, runtime-return evidence, and maintenance loops, but it shall not restore SDLC-local release ledgers, retry controllers, closure stores, or evidence admission as substitutes for GTL/ABG.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-009**: Ratified design shall classify release/readiness and feedback slots by upstream readiness. A slot that depends on missing, placeholder, test-only, unwired, or unpinned GTL/ABG substrate shall be deferred or blocked.

**REQ-GLC-RELEASE-OPERATIONAL-FEEDBACK-010**: Release/readiness and feedback queries shall be reproducible from pinned source or release identity plus admitted GTL/ABG replay/query truth.
