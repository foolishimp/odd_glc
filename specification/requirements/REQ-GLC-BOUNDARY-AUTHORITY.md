## Purpose

Define the authority boundary that makes `odd_glc` a lifecycle interpretation
product over GTL/ABG, not a second requirements algebra, runtime, closure
system, or software-domain method.

This family is load-bearing for T-001. Ratified graph design must descend from
this boundary before any build tenant or runtime implementation can be priced.

## Scope

This requirement governs the first Hello World lifecycle slice and future
downstream specialization.

`odd_glc` may define:

- lifecycle vocabulary;
- typed lifecycle assets;
- lifecycle meaning over GTL/ABG system-function carriers;
- read/query model interpretation;
- proof, fold, residual, and re-entry interpretation;
- downstream specialization contracts.

`odd_glc` may not define:

- a product-local requirement compiler;
- a writable requirement, evidence, closure, fold, residual, retry, or re-entry
  ledger;
- a product-local runtime loop, replay authority, continuation controller, or
  actor/process executor;
- generic software SDLC policy as lifecycle law.

## Acceptance Criteria

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-001**: `specification/` shall define `odd_glc` WHAT. `build_tenants/` and ratified design shall define HOW. Commentary, tickets, generated views, or sibling product precedent shall not outrank active specification.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-002**: GTL shall own graph, node, graph-vector, graph-function, module, job, interface, wrapper, and asset-surface declaration law consumed by `odd_glc`.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-003**: ABG shall own traversal, runtime facts, event replay, payload admission, evidence admission, assurance fold, continuation, residual, correction, and re-entry truth consumed by `odd_glc`.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-004**: `odd_glc` shall own lifecycle vocabulary, lifecycle meaning over GTL/ABG system-function carriers, policy overlays, read/query projections, proof interpretation, residual interpretation, and downstream specialization contracts over admitted GTL/ABG truth.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-005**: `odd_glc` lifecycle view assets shall be read models or interpretations. They shall not write GTL declaration truth or ABG runtime, admission, fold, residual, continuation, replay, or re-entry truth.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-006**: `odd_glc` lifecycle view surfaces shall be queries or labels over admitted GTL/ABG carriers when GTL/ABG already owns the carrier. They shall not become native typed carriers that shadow ABG `AuthorityContextFragment`, `EdgeRequirementEnvironment`, `DestinationTopology`, `RequirementEvidenceBinding`, `RequirementFoldProjection`, `RequirementResidualProjection`, continuation, or re-entry facts.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-007**: `RequirementSetAsset` shall author requirement pressure through GTL requirement-declaration carriers or wrappers. It shall reference and bind GTL declarations; it shall not mirror them in an `odd_glc`-native requirement representation that later requires translation into ABG terms.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-008**: Requirement authoring through `odd_glc` shall preserve source refs, source digests, relation refs, traversal span refs, context refs, evidence policy refs, and projection refs required by ABG requirements algebra admission.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-009**: Side-effecting execution for proof, including a Hello World program run, shall be an ABG-owned actor/operator edge. `odd_glc` may declare the required `CapabilityAsset` and interpret admitted evidence; it shall not shell out, supervise, retry, or admit the run as product-local authority.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-010**: `EvidenceBindingAsset` shall bind ABG-admitted evidence refs to active requirement projections. Local files, command success, stdout text, or harness assertions shall not close lifecycle pressure unless admitted through ABG and folded by ABG-owned assurance truth.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-011**: `AssuranceFoldViewAsset`, `ResidualPressureViewAsset`, and `ReentryDecisionAsset` shall interpret ABG-owned fold, residual, continuation, and re-entry facts. They shall not define product-local closure states, residual ledgers, retry routes, or continuation transitions.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-012**: Future `odd_sdlc` specialization shall consume `odd_glc` lifecycle contracts and may add software-domain assets or policy. It shall not push existing odd_sdlc phase names, local ledgers, retry loops, closure rules, or software policy back into generic `odd_glc` law.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-013**: Downstream programs that specialize `odd_glc` shall use GTL/ABG carriers and ABG runtime truth for graph structure, graph functions, execution, admission, evidence, fold, residual, continuation, replay, and re-entry. `odd_glc` shall not become a replacement graph catalog or runtime substrate.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-014**: T-001 slice-1 graph design may prove a forward Hello World lifecycle thread ending in one terminal disposition. General `odd_glc` design shall preserve cyclic re-entry semantics, where residual pressure can route through ABG re-entry facts back to an earlier lifecycle span.

**REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY-015**: Ratified graph design shall not be authored or accepted before the boundary-authority and ABG-consumption requirement families identify the lawful substrate and non-closure gates for each consuming lifecycle binding, query, or GTL composition slot.
