# REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE - Generic Mutable-Worksite Consumption

**Status**: Active

**Category**: Product Requirement / GTL-ABG Consumption

**Derives from**: [GOALS.md](../GOALS.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md),
[REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY](REQ-GLC-BOUNDARY-AUTHORITY.md), the Hello World
scenario ladder, and
[T-025](../../.ai-workspace/tickets/active/T-025-replay-scenario-ladder-as-typed-glc-declarations.md).

## Requirements

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-001**: Generic lifecycle declarations that
consume a changing worksite shall keep the ABI `WorkspaceBinding` distinct from
mutable `WorksiteObservation` evidence.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-002**: A reused scenario mutation edge shall bind
its subject, territory, current `O0`, selected ABI owner effect, owner receipt,
successor `O1`, ordinary C-call-result admission, and fresh replay relation.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-003**: A physical effect, process exit, worker
response, or fixture assertion alone shall not satisfy a generic lifecycle
edge. Admission refusal leaves visible residue and requires fresh observation;
it shall not infer rollback, success, or current `O1`.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-004**: Reused parallel scenario branches shall
declare disjoint territories before traversal. Fan-in shall consume admitted
branch results, not shared-worksite visibility.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-005**: Review is independent, non-mutating
evidence over exact candidate and evidence coordinates. It neither effects the
worksite nor admits runtime truth.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-006**: Hello World and Data Mapper acceptance
coverage shall reuse generic odd_glc declarations and their existing scenario
authority. Neither scenario shall be represented as a Product, Product member,
Program identity, or scenario-specific lifecycle catalogue entry.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-007**: [T-041 zero-code Product](../../build_tenants/common/design/ODD_GLC_ABI5_MIGRATION.md)
material may be consumed only through its existing declaration and installed-
Product relation. It shall not gain scenario-specific runtime or Product
identity.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-008**: Missing ABI owner, capability, territory,
admission, Event Calculus, or replay support shall block the dependent scenario
edge and record an ABI gap. `odd_glc` shall not implement a stage runner,
worker loop, filesystem owner, event writer, scheduler, retry controller, or
replay engine.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-009**: One reusable workflow declaration shall
carry lifecycle roles, dependencies, readiness, expected evidence, and return
relations for every selected scenario. Scenario data may vary subjects,
territories, inputs, and outcome predicates; it shall not vary the execution
mechanism or introduce a scenario-specific controller.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-010**: Live LLM execution shall use ABI-owned
Worker transport and traversal. Every invocation shall bind an exact actor,
model configuration, subject, work authority, evidence boundary, stop, and
closed return. A prompt is an activation projection and shall not become an
odd_glc workflow engine or authority source.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-011**: The selected acceptance population shall
include `basic-cli`, `js-tenant-test`, `js-sdlc-bootstrap`, `rust-cli`,
`rust-service`, `parallel-js`, and `data-mapper-full`. Data Mapper Lite may be
used as a bounded scout but shall not replace the full controlling scenario.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-012**: A discovered ABI defect shall preserve the
frozen scenario and return to the ABI owner. After the smallest lawful repair,
the rebuilt candidate shall rerun that same scenario before the Executive
advances the frontier. odd_glc shall not add compatibility logic around the
defect.

**REQ-GLC-WORKSITE-LIFECYCLE-013**: Every declared subject command and outcome
probe shall execute inside a typed ABI-owned `F_P` Worker turn over the admitted
worksite state. The return shall bind the ordered command results and exactly
one typed observation for every declared outcome predicate. odd_glc may apply
deterministic domain proof interpretation to those admitted observations; its
framework or harness shall not spawn the subject toolchain, import a subject
module, perform a subject HTTP request, or synthesize an observation from a
declaration.
