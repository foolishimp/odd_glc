## 2.3.0 Decisions Applied Here

The following additions are material to current and future work in this repo:

1. **Transformation admission completeness (`STDO-UP-016`)**. A consequential
   lifecycle transformation must identify its complete admission relation,
   not merely name ABG, GTL, F_P, F_H, or another actor. Projection state
   cannot authenticate its own admission. Every equivalent competing path is
   dispositioned as constituent, replica, replacement, or deliberately
   independent realization.
2. **Bounded frame conservation (`STDO-UP-017`)**. A change declares its
   governing frames, causally closed affected relation set, inherited
   invariants, and crossing authority/composition seams. A far-side relation is
   included when causally relevant or is explicitly proven unable to
   participate. Local realization conserves governing truth; changing that
   truth re-enters at its owner.
3. **Invariant reconstruction sufficiency (`STDO-UP-018`)**. The selected STDO
   basis plus accepted design must let a fresh bounded actor reconstruct every
   governing relation in the affected set and decide whether the action
   preserves, changes, duplicates, or violates it without conversation,
   commentary, prior-agent memory, or implementation folklore.
4. **Computational realization projection (`STDO-UP-019`)**. Material design
   projects lifecycle meaning into data structures, identity/key structures,
   algorithms, algebraic laws, effect boundaries, implementations, and
   domain-to-computational mappings. Each component has an explicit semantic
   and operational role set. Similar code is a commonization candidate, not
   evidence of shared semantic ownership; every material algorithmic
   obligation receives both semantic and realization disposition.
5. **Agentic construction execution (`STDO-UP-020`)**. Delegated construction
   starts from a reconstruction-sufficient accepted basis and the smallest
   coherent causal cone. The work item binds affected relations, delegated
   authority, assessment, re-entry conditions, and exact transition evidence.
   Local repair is proportional; Product, requirement, governing-authority, or
   accepted-design changes re-enter at their owning relation.
