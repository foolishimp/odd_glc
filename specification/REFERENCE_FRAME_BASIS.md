## Product Roles And Fixed Identity

`odd_glc` is one mutable Source Project building one generic lifecycle Product.
Hello World and Data Mapper are scenario and test subjects, never additional
odd_glc Products, Product members, Program identities or lifecycle engines.
The accepted T-041 cut is immutable predecessor evidence. ABIogenesis is the
separate runtime substrate: its source candidate may be used for bounded
dogfood, but no ABG candidate becomes an odd_glc release dependency or gains
odd_glc meaning.

The complete selected application specification has a separate user/input role.
Its `IntentAsset` and `ProductDefinitionAsset` describe the application subject;
they do not replace the builder Product Definition or inherit odd_sdlc runtime
authority. Source members, digests, modalities, contradictions and unresolved
references are evaluation inputs. An inventory proves source identity, not a
complete semantic decomposition. Candidate/source hashes and current activation
are recorded in T-043 and its bounded evidence record.

## Evaluation And Decision Ownership

Computations (C) check admitted source/predecessor identities, obligation and
evidence-role congruence, declared coverage, currentness and typed refusals.
Agents supply judgments (J) of faithful decomposition, material interactions,
proof sufficiency and unresolved source pressure. J remains candidate evidence
until admitted under the applicable contract. Owners (O) retain scope,
conflict and acceptance rulings within their grants. None substitutes for the
others, and none creates a GLC runtime or peer requirement/proof ledger.

Realization and proof evaluations must meet on the same admitted obligation,
source and current basis. A semantic Reviewer must be able to reach the full
source, predecessor result and actual candidate/evidence rather than only a
generated synopsis or successful test count. A computationally complete table
does not establish that its obligation set faithfully represents the input.

## Frame And Actor Binding

The selected generic STDO frames are Product, Design, Public Boundary, Effect,
Owner, Proof, Executive, Worker, and Reviewer. A scenario-specific testing
frame may refine Proof but cannot create another Product identity.

`Writer` is the local name for a Worker activation allowed to make bounded
candidate changes. The Writer returns exact changed paths, verification,
residuals, and the Executive route. The Reviewer receives the frozen exact
subject, evaluates without editing, assigns technical severity and triage, and
returns only to Executive. Executive owns attention scheduling, priority,
current-boundary effect, disposition, and the next already-authorized action.

## Technical Triage And Promotion Policy

Reviewer severity descends from `S0` through `S4`: `S0` is catastrophic or a
non-waivable authority, integrity, safety, or data-loss failure; `S1` loses a
mandatory Product outcome or hard stop without an adequate workaround; `S2`
is a material semantic, causal, authority, proportionality, or proof defect
with a bounded workaround; `S3` is a minor ambiguity or evidence defect; and
`S4` is editorial. Unsupported assessment is `indeterminate`; no finding is
`not_applicable`.

Executive priority descends from `P0` through `P5`. `P0` through `P2` block the
current MVP boundary until repaired, lawfully narrowed from the claim, or
retained by an authority permitted to accept the risk. `P3` through `P5` are
retained but do not block this boundary. No mapping waives STDO hard stops or
turns an adjacent out-of-claim observation into a blocker.

## Executive Attention, Evaluation, And Action

The Executive keeps one finite frontier over the dependency order:

```text
exact full input, lifecycle declaration and selected bounded outcome
  -> one dependency-ready Writer activation
  -> closed authority, implementation or execution result for that grant
  -> one independent Reviewer activation over the frozen candidate and result
  -> Executive priority and boundary disposition
  -> next obligation | affected repair/resume | stop and re-enter
```

The Executive never joins every frame into one actor context. It selects the
smallest reconstructable activation sufficient for the next decision and
conjoins only declared seams: selected source with semantic derivation;
requirement with its paired realization/proof; changed basis with affected
invalidation; generic Product identity with ABG binding; and ABG causal truth
with the application outcome. Conflicts and residuals remain visible. This
engagement topology is not a host implementation of lifecycle traversal.

## Request And Return Shape

Each activation is structured prose with: role and outcome; exact subject and
frame; hard constraints; material evidence and source routes; open solution
space; return and stop contract; and `ACTION` last. It is not a generated
workflow or schema. Anything not prohibited remains available to the capable
actor.

Every activation returns one closed STDO result. A model response, process
exit, changed file, or test assertion is evidence, not automatic admission,
acceptance, priority, or continuation.

## Drift Locks And Source Re-entry

Re-enter at the owning authority if Product identity, scenario authority,
method cut, ABG composition contract, workflow topology, operation grant,
worksite binding, evidence basis, actor capability, or current MVP mandate
changes. Resolve `stdo://` sources through the verified RC4 install and local
Product sources through exact paths and a frozen worktree checkpoint. A stale
map, summary, prompt, test, comment, or historical campaign never replaces its
owning source.

A source or requirement change identifies affected prior derivations, design,
artifacts, evidence and frame results; those claims require reevaluation.
Unchanged results retain only their demonstrated basis and scope. Earlier
campaign results keep their frozen frame/source basis and bounded acceptance;
the expanded lifecycle declaration grants them no new semantic coverage.
